About me

My research focuses on theoretical as well as applied probability, with particular interests in stochastic analysis, random graphs, stochastic geometry, and extreme value theory.

More recently, my research has focused on developing probabilistic frameworks for the mathematical analysis of chemical reaction networks and the chemical origins of life.

Research Directions

Probabilistic Models of Chemical Systems

The mathematical foundations of the chemical origins of life.

Random Spatial Structures

The study of geometric and probabilistic phenomena in random spatial systems.

Stochastic Analysis of Fractional Brownian Motion

Understanding stochastic processes and dynamics with long-range dependence.
