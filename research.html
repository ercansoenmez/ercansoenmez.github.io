Research

Probabilistic Models of Chemical Systems

The mathematical foundations of the chemical origins of life.

My research focuses on developing probabilistic frameworks for the mathematical analysis of the chemical origins of life. A central question is under which conditions self-sustaining chemical reaction networks can emerge from systems of interacting molecules.

I am interested in the development and rigorous analysis of mathematical models of chemical reaction networks, with particular emphasis on autocatalysis, phase transitions, and the emergence of self-sustaining chemical systems.

My recent work investigates how heterogeneity in catalytic activity influences the emergence of autocatalytic networks, and whether self-sustaining chemical systems can arise through the activity of only a few highly effective catalysts.

Origin of life · Chemical reaction networks · Autocatalysis · RAF theory · Phase transitions · Stochastic biology

Random Spatial Structures

The study of geometric and probabilistic phenomena in random spatial systems.

My research focuses on the mathematical understanding of random spatial structures in discrete and continuous settings.

My work combines methods from random graph theory, percolation theory, stochastic geometry, and extreme value theory to investigate connectivity, phase transitions, and the emergence of extreme geometric configurations.

My recent research includes the study of long-range percolation models, extreme edge lengths in spatial random graphs, and intersection probabilities of random geometric objects in hyperbolic space.

Random graphs · Percolation theory · Stochastic geometry · Extreme value theory · Long-range interactions · Phase transitions · Hyperbolic geometry

Stochastic Analysis of Fractional Brownian Motion

Understanding stochastic processes and dynamics with long-range dependence.

My research focuses on fractional Brownian motion with Hurst parameter H > 1/2, a fundamental stochastic process for modelling phenomena exhibiting long-range dependence. I aim to advance the mathematical understanding of this process and of stochastic differential equations driven by it, with particular emphasis on regularization by noise and the regularity properties of their solutions.

My recent work includes the development of Tanaka-type formulas, strong well-posedness results for stochastic differential equations with irregular coefficients, density estimates, and the analysis of local time properties.

Fractional Brownian motion · Long-range dependence · Singular SDEs · Regularization by noise · Strong well-posedness · Tanaka-type formulas · Density estimates · Local times
